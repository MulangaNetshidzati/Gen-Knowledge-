      🎯
      How to Play Letter Rush
      📝
      26 rounds, one for each letter (A–Z)

      Each round shows a random letter. Fill in all 6 categories with words that start with that letter.

      ⏱️
      60 seconds per round — strict!

      When the timer hits zero, whatever you've typed is locked in. No waiting for slow players.

      ✅
      +10 points per correct answer

      Your word must start with the round letter AND be a real entry in that category.

      ⚠️
      −5 penalty per matching answer

      If another player gives the same correct word, you both lose 5 points for each match. Think of something original!

      🪄
      Auto-correct is on

      Small typos are fixed automatically (e.g. toyata → toyota). You'll see a green flash when a word is corrected.

      🏆
      Highest score after 26 rounds wins

      The leaderboard updates after every round — watch the crown change hands!
